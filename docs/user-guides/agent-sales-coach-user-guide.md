# Ruoff Agent Sales Coach  
### User Guide for Real Estate Agents

**Live app:** [https://ruoffagentsalescoach.onrender.com](https://ruoffagentsalescoach.onrender.com)

---

## 1. What this tool is for

The **Agent Sales Coach** helps realtors run a clearer, more consistent week: planning, listings, open houses, content, bios, newsletters, and relationship touches—in one place.

Use it to:

- Plan your week with protected time and specific tasks  
- Write listing copy and open house scripts faster  
- Stay visible with social posts, blogs, and newsletters  
- Build a bio that sounds like you  
- Find pop-by ideas, gifts, and nurture scripts  
- Ask an AI Coach when you need a second brain  

It is built for **working agents**, not corporate planners. Fill your profile once; the tools pull from it so output sounds like you.

## 2. How to get access and log in

### Get an invite

Access is **invite-only**. Your Ruoff loan officer (or an admin) creates an invite for you.

Typical path:

1. Your LO sends you an invite link (often looks like the app URL with `#invite=…`).  
2. Open the link on the device you’ll use most.  
3. Accept the invite and create your password.  
4. Sign in.

You can also open the app and choose **Accept invite** or **Request access** if you don’t have a link yet.

### Sign in

1. Go to [https://ruoffagentsalescoach.onrender.com](https://ruoffagentsalescoach.onrender.com).  
2. Enter your email and password.  
3. Check **Remember this device (30 days)** on a personal or work laptop you trust.  
4. Click **Sign in**.

### Sign out

Open **My Profile** (header) → use **Sign out** in the account area when you’re on a shared computer.

### Tip

Use the **same browser and device** for demos and daily work when possible. Remembered sessions reduce login friction.

## 3. Home screen walkthrough

Home is your launchpad. Everything important starts here.

### Top of Home

- **Greeting** — personalized when your profile has a name  
- **Setup status chips** — quick cues that profile pieces are ready  
- **Open AI Coach** — jump into a conversation

If you see a setup checklist, finish it first. Priority fields:

- Name  
- Market / location  
- Focus and hours  
- Tone / voice  

A stronger profile = better Weekly Plans, bios, newsletters, and Coach answers.

### Favorites

Pin tools you use often so they sit near the top of Home.

A compact path for “what should I do today?”:

1. **Weekly Win Plan** — plan the week  
2. **Value Vault** — pick a touch or gift idea  
3. **Database** — nurture the right people  
4. **Content Studio** — create content  
5. **Listing Copy** — write MLS-ready descriptions  
6. **Open House** — prep scripts and follow-up  

One card may show **Recommended for you** based on your profile focus.

### Jump in

Shortcuts to tools you’ll use most (Bio, Listing, Open House, Consult, Content, Newsletter, Business Plan, and more).

### Navigation

- **Sidebar** (menu) — all tools by category  
- **Search** (header) — find tools and content by keyword  
- **My Profile** — your identity, market, goals, branding  
- **My Saved Items** — anything you saved from generators

## 5. Recommended first-week workflow

Do this once, then repeat a lighter version every week.

### Day 1 — Set the foundation (20–30 min)

1. Sign in and open **My Profile**.  
2. Complete: name, market, company, phone/email, focus, hours, tone.  
3. Open **Bio Builder** → generate → **Save as Primary Bio**.  
4. Star 4–5 **Favorites** on Home (Weekly Win, Value Vault, Listing, Newsletter, AI Coach).

### Day 2 — Plan the week (15–20 min)

1. Open **Weekly Win Plan**.  
2. Confirm hours and this week’s focus (sphere, past clients, listings, buyers, open houses).  
3. Click **Build This Week's Plan**.  
4. Put the protected blocks on your real calendar.

### Day 3 — Deliver one value touch (15 min)

1. Open **Value Vault** → **Pop-Bys**.  
2. Copy one idea and a short note.  
3. Deliver it within 48 hours.  
4. Log the touch in your Weekly Win Plan.

### Day 4 — Create one piece of content (20–30 min)

Pick one:

- **Posts & calendar** for social, or  
- **Newsletter** (write a real Personal Update first—at least a few sentences), or  
- **Listing Copy** if you have an active listing  

Save winners to **My Saved Items**.

### Day 5 — Opportunity day (20–30 min)

- Prep an **Open House Kit**, or  
- Run a **Consult** kit for an upcoming appointment, or  
- Ask **AI Coach** for objection help before a tough conversation  

### Weekend / next Monday

Review what you actually finished. Rebuild Weekly Win Plan if the week changed. Consistency beats perfection.

## 6. Tips for best results

### Profile first

Generators personalize from **My Profile**. Empty name/market/tone = generic output.

### Be specific in the form

Good: “4 bed in Homestead, new roof 2024, fenced yard for dogs, walk to trails.”  
Weak: “Nice house.”

### One primary action per session

On a busy day, do **one** of: plan, vault touch, listing, or newsletter—not all four cold.

### Newsletter rule

A **Personal Update** is required before generate. Write something real (family, local win, market observation). Generic “rates and inventory” alone underperforms.

### Save what works

Use **My Saved Items** and **Primary Bio** so you don’t regenerate the same quality from scratch.

### Review AI output

Treat every generate as a strong first draft. Add one personal line before MLS, email, or social.

### Loading is normal

Some tools load on first open and generations can take ~30–60 seconds. Stay on the screen; don’t rapid-click Generate.

## 7. Common questions and troubleshooting

### I can’t get in

- Confirm you accepted an invite for **this** app (Agent Sales Coach, not the LO tool).  
- Try **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen.  
- Ask your LO to resend or create a new invite.  
- Request access from the gate if you don’t have an invite yet.

### The page looks blank or stuck on login

- Hard refresh (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + R).  
- Confirm you’re on [ruoffagentsalescoach.onrender.com](https://ruoffagentsalescoach.onrender.com).  
- Try another browser or turn off aggressive ad blockers for this site.

### Generate button won’t start (Bio)

Orange **essential** fields usually need to be filled first. Complete name/market/story basics, then try again.

### Newsletter won’t generate

Write a longer **Personal Update** (aim for a real paragraph). The tool blocks generate until that update is substantial enough.

### Results don’t sound like me

- Update **My Profile** tone, market, and intro.  
- Save a **Primary Bio**.  
- Add custom notes on the tool form (specifics beat adjectives).  
- Regenerate once after those updates.

### I don’t see Mortgage Calculator

That tool is intentionally not part of the Agent experience. Use your LO partner for financing scenarios; focus here on listing, content, and relationships.

### Something failed mid-generate

Wait for the overlay to finish or clear. Try once more. If it fails again, use a saved draft from **My Saved Items** or ask **AI Coach** for a shorter version while you retry later.

### Who do I contact?

Start with the Ruoff loan officer who invited you. Use **Send feedback** in the sidebar for product feedback when available.
