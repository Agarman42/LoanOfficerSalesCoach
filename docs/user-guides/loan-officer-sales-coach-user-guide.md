## 1. What this tool is for

The **Loan Officer Sales Coach** is your daily operating system for production: planning the week, finding refinance and equity opportunities, running client meetings, creating content, and inviting realtor partners into the Agent Sales Coach.

Use it to:

- Build a realistic Weekly Win Plan around your hours and goals  
- Scan for equity / refinance opportunities  
- Prepare Smart Savings and mortgage scenarios for conversations  
- Stay visible with social, blog, and newsletter content  
- Keep a Primary Bio and sales scripts ready  
- Invite realtors and share a branded partner experience  
- Ask AI Coach when you need scripts, objections, or strategy  

Fill **My Profile** once. Plans, content, and Coach answers personalize from it.

## 2. How to get access and log in

### Who can use it

This app is for **Ruoff loan officers** (and admins). Sign-in expects your **@ruoff.com** email.

### Create account / sign in

1. Go to [https://loanofficersalescoach.onrender.com](https://loanofficersalescoach.onrender.com).  
2. Create an account or sign in with your Ruoff email.  
3. Check **Remember this device** on a trusted work laptop.  
4. Complete **My Profile** early (name, NMLS, markets, goals, headshot if you have one).

### Invite a realtor partner

From Home (Partners card) or the sidebar invite path:

1. Create an invite for your realtor.  
2. Share the invite link.  
3. When they accept, they get **Agent Sales Coach** access—and can see your LO branding where configured.

One invite path covers account access; optional short partner links are for people already on the Agent tool.

### Sign out

Open **My Profile** → **Sign out** when using a shared machine.

## 5. Recommended first-week workflow

### Day 1 — Foundation (25–35 min)

1. Sign in with your **@ruoff.com** email.  
2. Complete **My Profile** (name, NMLS, markets, goals, hours, tone, headshot).  
3. Generate a bio in **Bio Builder** → **Save as Primary**.  
4. Invite **one** realtor partner from Home.  
5. Favorite: Weekly Win, Equity Scanner, Smart Savings, Value Vault, Newsletter, AI Coach.

### Day 2 — Plan the week (15–20 min)

1. Open **Weekly Win Plan**.  
2. Confirm hours and focus.  
3. **Build This Week's Plan**.  
4. Block the power hours on your real calendar (including partner and database touches).

### Day 3 — Opportunity outreach (20–30 min)

1. Open **Equity Scanner** (upload a list or use demo data to learn the flow).  
2. Pick your top opportunities.  
3. Generate a **Sales Script** for the first call.  
4. Log follow-ups in Weekly Win Plan.

### Day 4 — Meeting prep (15–25 min)

1. Run **Smart Savings** (or Mortgage Calculator) for a real conversation.  
2. Save the scenario view you like.  
3. Optional: draft an LOE if a file needs language.

### Day 5 — Visibility (20–30 min)

Pick one content move:

- **Social Post & Calendar**, or  
- **Newsletter** (write a real Personal Update first), or  
- **Blog Creator** for a local authority piece  

Save keepers to **My Saved Items**.

### Ongoing

- One **Value Vault** touch per week minimum  
- One realtor invite or partner follow-up per week  
- Rebuild Weekly Win every Monday (or whenever the week changes)

## 6. Tips for best results

### Profile powers everything

Weekly Plans, bios, newsletters, and Coach answers all read **My Profile**. Incomplete profile = flat output.

### Specific beats generic

In scripts and content forms, add real context: city, partner name, payment pain, timeline. One concrete detail changes the quality.

### Equity → script → calendar

Don’t stop at the Equity Scanner dashboard. Choose leads, generate language, then protect call time in Weekly Win Plan.

### Smart Savings in the meeting

Build A/B/C scenarios before or during the appointment. Save or copy the comparison so the client leaves with clarity.

### Newsletter Personal Update

Write something human first (family, local market note, client win). The generator expects a real personal update before it builds the rest.

### Partner invites are part of production

Inviting realtors isn’t “admin work”—it’s pipeline. Use the Home Partners path regularly.

### Review before you send

AI drafts are strong first passes. Add your judgment before email, social, or underwriting language goes out.

### Generations take a moment

First open of a tool may load briefly. Generates often take ~30–60 seconds. Stay on the page until the overlay finishes.

## 7. Common questions and troubleshooting

### I can’t create an account / sign in

- Use your **@ruoff.com** email.  
- Try **Forgot password**.  
- Confirm you’re on [loanofficersalescoach.onrender.com](https://loanofficersalescoach.onrender.com) (not the Agent realtor URL).  
- Contact an admin if your account was deactivated.

### My realtor can’t get into Agent Sales Coach

- Confirm you created the invite from **this** LO app.  
- Resend or create a fresh invite.  
- Have them open the invite link on mobile Safari/Chrome and accept before bookmarking.  
- Agent access is separate from LO login—they need the Agent URL.

### Equity Scanner looks empty

Upload a spreadsheet/export, or load demo data to learn the screens. Then prioritize high-equity / high-gap opportunities first.

### Smart Savings / Calculator feels crowded on a small screen

Use a laptop for client meetings when possible. Save scenarios and present the comparison view, not every input field.

### Newsletter won’t generate

Add a longer **Personal Update** with real detail. Guided setup helps if you’re starting from zero.

### Bio Generate stays disabled

Complete essential profile/bio fields (name, market, story basics). Essentials drive most of the quality.

### Content doesn’t sound like Ruoff / like me

Update tone, markets, and Primary Bio. Add content guardrails in Profile if you have phrases to avoid. Regenerate once after saving.

### Admin questions (usage, passwords, invites)

Admins: use **Admin · Usage** in the sidebar for user status, login activity, and invite oversight.

### Who do I contact?

Your team admin or leadership for access issues. Use **Send feedback** in the sidebar for product suggestions.
